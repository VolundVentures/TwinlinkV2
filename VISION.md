# TwinLink Director — Vision

**For directors, not marketers.** The product is a studio where a filmmaker briefs a director persona and approves a film stage by stage. No live avatar, no film-school layer: the persona's craft shows up in the work, not in a lecture.

## Principles

- **Scenes, not clips.** The unit is a 25-second Seedance 2.5 scene with native sound. Cuts come from coverage, not from chopping.
- **One provider.** Everything renders through Runway. One key, one bill, one rate limiter.
- **Frame anchor.** Identity is locked in plates, composed into a keyframe, then moved for 25 seconds. Prompts describe time; keyframes describe space.
- **Approval gates.** Treatment, plates, keyframes, scenes, final cut. The director proposes; the user decides.
